About us

Change starts within communities.

Lead Now Foundation is a youth-centred organisation based in Delhi. We exist to build the leadership, skills and confidence of young people as active contributors to development.

Who we are

Built with communities, never for them.

We work where youth leadership, community transformation and lasting change meet. Every programme, strategy and solution is built with the young people and communities we serve.

We believe the world's greatest untapped resource is the leadership potential of young people, particularly those who have been overlooked and underestimated. Our job is to create the conditions in which they can do extraordinary things themselves.

Young people are the most credible architects of their communities' futures. We see them as leaders, co-creators, innovators and changemakers, and we design everything around that belief.

Vision

Thriving, inclusive and resilient communities shaped by young people who lead collective action and influence systems for lasting social change.

Mission

To build the leadership, skills and agency of young people to contribute to community transformation and positive change, as co-creators of their own futures.

What we stand for

Five principles. One direction.

These values are the decisions we make every day: how we run programmes, treat partners, spend money and speak about communities. They exist to be lived.

Youth Agency

Young people lead, decide and create, rather than simply participate. Their voice is the decision-making authority, not a consultation exercise.

Equity

We pay closest attention to those most often left behind. Equity means giving everyone what they need to thrive, rather than treating everyone the same.

Collaboration

We build movements, not empires, with partners and peers. No single organisation has the full answer. Working together is the strategy.

Dignity

Every person and every community deserves respect and care. It shapes every word we write, every decision we make and every relationship we hold.

Systems Thinking

We look beyond quick fixes toward lasting, structural change. The root causes of injustice are systemic, so the solutions must be too.

Governance

Registered, accountable, transparent.

We are governed by a Board of Directors and registered as a not-for-profit company in Delhi.
