Our Three Priority Areas

Everything we do flows through these three lenses. They are intersecting, not isolated.

💻

Digital Learning and Innovation

We harness technology as a force for equity. From coding workshops in government schools to digital safety training for parents, we build the skills and confidence young people need to navigate, create, and lead in a digital world. We ensure access for those most at risk of being left behind.

📚

Education and Life Skills

Education is more than curriculum. We equip young people with the knowledge, critical thinking, and practical capabilities to navigate complex realities, advocate for themselves, and contribute to their communities. Learning that is relevant, participatory, and transformative.

⚖️

Mental Health and Gender Equality

We address the intersecting challenges that hold young people back. From challenging gender norms in classrooms to building community understanding of mental wellbeing, we create safe spaces where every young person, especially girls and those most marginalised, can thrive and lead.

Who We Are

A youth-centered organization built on the belief that change starts within communities.

Lead Now Foundation is a registered Section 8 company based in Delhi, India. We exist to build the leadership, skills, and collective power of young people, positioning them not as recipients of development, but as its architects.

We work at the intersection of youth leadership, community transformation, and systems change. Our approach is grounded in co-creation: every programme, every strategy, every solution is built with the communities we serve, never for them.

Vision

A world where young people lead the change their communities deserve, not as a future aspiration, but as a present reality.

Mission

Lead Now Foundation builds the leadership, skills, and power of young people to drive community transformation and systems change, with young people not as beneficiaries, but as co-creators of their own futures.

Tagline

"Building Leaders. Transforming Communities."

The name is the call to action. The future is the promise.

Our Thinking

Why we exist and how we understand change.

A deep belief that young people are the world's most underutilised force for transformation.

We believe the world's greatest untapped resource is the leadership potential of young people, particularly those who have been overlooked, excluded, and underestimated.

Lead Now Foundation exists not to do things for young people, but to create the conditions in which they can do extraordinary things themselves. We reject the idea that young people are problems to be solved, or populations to be managed. We see them as the most credible architects of their own communities' futures.

We understand that individual transformation and systemic change are not separate, they are deeply linked. When one young person leads, others follow. When communities organise, systems shift. And when young people are genuinely resourced and trusted, nothing is out of reach.

Our work begins at the intersection of leadership and belonging, where a young person first discovers that their voice matters, their ideas have power, and their community needs them. From that moment, we walk alongside them.

NURTURE

Invest in untapped potential

We invest in the leadership potential of young people, especially those historically marginalised. Tailored training, workshops, and mentorship that meets each person where they are.

CONNECT

Build collective power

The collective power of young people is a formidable force. We build connections across communities, civil society, and networks, breaking barriers and amplifying voices.

RESOURCE

Fund what matters

We raise and channel resources to young leaders and youth-led organisations, so that their priorities, not ours, drive the agenda. Flexible funding for flexible change.

INFLUENCE

Shift systems and power

We work to change the structures and systems that determine who leads and who is listened to. Advocacy, policy engagement, and institutional accountability at every level.

What We Stand For

Eight principles. One direction.

These values are not aspirational statements pinned to a wall. They are the decisions we make every day: how we run programmes, how we treat partners, how we spend money, and how we talk about the communities we work with.

They exist to be challenged, not just celebrated.

01

Youth Agency

Young people lead, decide and create, not just participate. Their voice is not a consultation mechanism. It is the decision-making authority.

02

Co-Creation

Every programme, strategy and solution is built with communities, not for them. There is no product here that doesn't carry the fingerprints of those it serves.

03

Equity

We name and address power, especially for those most excluded. Equity is not about treating everyone the same. It is about giving everyone what they need.

04

Community Ownership

Change led from within, not imposed from outside. The most sustainable transformation is the kind a community builds, owns, and believes in.

05

Learning Culture

We adapt, question and grow, including from failure. The willingness to be wrong is a prerequisite for getting things right.

06

Collaboration

We build movements, not empires, with partners and peers. No single organisation has the full answer. Working together is not a compromise; it is the strategy.

07

Dignity

Every person and every community deserves respect and care. This is not performative. It shapes every word we write, every decision we make, every relationship we hold.

08

Systems Thinking

We look beyond quick fixes toward lasting, structural change. The root causes of injustice are systemic. The solutions must be too.

How Change Happens

A hypothesis, not a blueprint.

Our Theory of Change reflects our perspective on how youth-led change occurs, and how we seek to initiate, support, and sustain it. We acknowledge that change is not a straightforward process. It is complex, layered, and interconnected.

Our hypothesis is simple: support the collective power of young leaders and youth civil society to create a better world, and they will improve outcomes. This is not blind optimism. It is a conclusion drawn from evidence, experience, and deep respect for what young people are already doing, often without recognition or resource.

The Change Pathway

Nurture Young Leaders

Invest in the largely untapped leadership potential of young people, especially those historically marginalised, through tailored training, mentorship, and support.

Connect Youth Civil Society

Build connections across communities, networks, and movements, enabling young people to learn from each other, build collective power, and amplify their voices.

Resource Youth-Led Action

Channel flexible, unrestricted resources to young leaders and youth-led organisations, so their priorities drive the agenda, not ours.

Influence Systems

Work to shift the structures and systems that determine who leads, who is funded, and who is listened to, from local governance to national policy.

Levels of Change

Individual Change

Increased confidence, agency, and leadership skills. Stronger peer networks, improved decision-making power, and positive outcomes on priority issues. Each young person's leadership journey is unique.

Community Change

Communities are more cohesive, better resourced, and better able to address issues that matter to them, with young people as active participants and leaders, not objects of development.

Collective & Sectoral Change

Youth civil society is connected, resilient, funded, and thriving. Development actors increasingly value and invest in youth-led approaches. The field recognises what young people can do.

Systemic Change

Systems are changed for the better as young people use their collective voice to influence decision-makers, drive accountability, and shape policies that reflect their priorities.
