Youth-centred social impact · New Delhi

Young people are not beneficiaries. They are leaders.

Lead Now Foundation builds the leadership, skills and agency of young people, so they can co-create the futures of their communities and help shape the systems around them.

Where we focus

Three priority areas

Each area is a place where young people already feel the pressure of change, and where their leadership can shift what happens next.

Digital futures

Digital Learning, AI and Innovation

Skills that go beyond screens, from digital literacy and coding to understanding AI. Young people become critical thinkers and makers in an AI-shaped world, rather than passive consumers of it.

Project: Lead Forward
Planet

Climate Action and Resilience

Young people are the most affected by the climate crisis and the most determined to confront it. We help them read their local environment, connect it to global systems and build responses their communities can sustain.

Wellbeing and equity

Mental Health and Gender Equality

We challenge gender norms, build mental wellbeing literacy and create safe spaces where every young person, especially girls and those most marginalised, can grow, lead and belong.

How change happens

From the inside out. From the ground up.

Our Theory of Change follows one young person's spark as it moves outward into communities and institutions.

  1. 1

    The spark

    A young person discovers that their voice matters.

  2. 2

    Individual leadership

    Confidence, skills and agency grow through experience, mentoring and peer learning.

  3. 3

    Community impact

    Young leaders mobilise peers and co-create solutions for their streets, schools and neighbourhoods.

  4. 4

    Lasting change

    Youth leadership becomes part of how institutions decide, fund and govern.
