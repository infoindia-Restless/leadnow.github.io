Our work

Where young people lead the change.

We focus on three areas where young people feel change most sharply, and four ways of working that put resources and decisions in their hands.

Priority
area
Digital futures

Digital Learning, AI and Innovation

We equip young people with skills that go beyond screens, from digital literacy and coding to understanding how AI works and where it falls short. Through hands-on workshops and innovation challenges, they learn to question technology, build with it and use it for their communities.

  • Digital literacy, coding and AI awareness
  • Hands-on workshops and innovation challenges
  • Critical thinking about technology and its impact
Priority areaLocal roots,
global systems.
Planet

Climate Action and Resilience

Young people are the most affected by the climate crisis and the most determined to confront it. We support them to understand their local environment, connect it to global systems and build community-led responses that are practical and sustained.

  • Understanding local environments and risks
  • Youth-led community action projects
  • Campaigns and collective action for resilience
Priority
area
Wellbeing and equity

Mental Health and Gender Equality

We challenge gender norms, build mental wellbeing literacy and create safe spaces where every young person, especially girls and those most marginalised, can grow, lead and belong.

  • Mental wellbeing literacy and peer support
  • Safe spaces for girls and marginalised young people
  • Challenging harmful gender norms with communities
Our approach

Why we exist and how we understand change.

When one young person leads, others follow. When communities come together, things shift. When young people are trusted, the change they make lasts.

NURTURE

Invest in untapped potential

Tailored training, workshops and mentorship that meet each young person where they are, especially those historically marginalised.

CONNECT

Build collective voice

We build bridges across communities, civil society and networks, so young people can collaborate, support one another and be heard.

RESOURCE

Fund what matters

We raise and channel resources to young leaders and youth-led organisations, so their priorities drive the agenda.

INFLUENCE

Engage with systems and policy

We support young people to engage with institutions, policies and decision-making spaces, so their perspectives count at every level.
